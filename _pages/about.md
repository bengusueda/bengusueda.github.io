I am a Ph.D. candidate in **Materials Science and Engineering** at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**, advised by [Cecilia Leal](https://cecilial.matse.illinois.edu/).

My research focuses on **lipid-peptide hybrid membrane systems, biomimetic membranes, and the biophysical characterization of soft matter**. I study how these systems are structured, how they respond mechanically, and how they function, with broader implications for biomimetic cell membrane design and selective separation membranes.

I characterize membranes using atomic force microscopy (AFM), small-angle X-ray scattering (SAXS), confocal laser scanning microscopy (CLSM), and cryo-electron microscopy. I received my B.Sc. in Materials Science and Nano Engineering from Sabancı University, with minors in Chemistry and Physics.
