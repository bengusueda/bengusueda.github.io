## Biomimetic membranes & soft matter

My work examines lipid-peptide hybrid membranes, their response to temperature, and the relationship between membrane structure, mechanical properties, and function. My broader research experience spans polymer nanocarriers, biomedical coatings, bacterial cellulose composites, and biosensors.

## Laboratory & computational skills

**Characterization:** Atomic force microscopy; small/wide-angle X-ray scattering; cryogenic electron microscopy; fluorescence correlation spectroscopy; confocal microscopy; single particle profiler; small-angle neutron scattering.

**Fabrication & biology:** Blow spinning, electrospinning, initiated chemical vapor deposition (iCVD), and cell culture.

**Programming:** Python and MATLAB.

**Data analysis & design:** AutoCAD, Gwyddion, Igor, and BioXTAS RAW.
